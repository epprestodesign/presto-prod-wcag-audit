Hotel Cancellation Policy

Reservations may be changed or cancelled until 48 hours prior to arrival without penalty. Changes, including shortened stays, and/or cancellations received after 48 hours prior to arrival will assess a one night’s room and tax penalty. Please note: All booking fees are non-refundable, including those for waitlist reservations and multiple bookings. If you anticipate being a late arrival, please be sure to contact the hotel the day prior to that arrival so that they know. IMPORTANT: All modifications and cancellations must go through Team Travel Source, you can use the links in your email receipt or contact Team Travel Source via phone at 502.354.9103. NOTE: You will receive a pre-arrival email leading up to your arrival instructing you to contact the hotel directly with all modifications and cancellations. From that point forward, you will need to reach out to the hotel directly for any modifications or cancellations.
